# VoiceCV — 3-Minute Demo Video Shooting Script

> **For:** Kanz AI Hackathon (July 13–20, 2026) judge submission.
> **Format:** Screen recording + your English voiceover. Target **3:00** (hard cap — judges often stop at 3:00).
> **Live URL:** https://vocv.app · **Brand:** DevVibe AI
>
> Each scene has: **[timecode]**, 🎬 what's on screen (shot list), 🎙️ word-for-word narration, and 💬 the on-screen caption (use these if you go voiceless). Narration is written for ~140 words/min — read at a calm, confident pace.

---

## The arc (why this order wins)
Problem → Solution → **See it work (voice → CV)** → It's real & polished (edit + PDF) → It goes further (Job Match) → Impact + call to action. Judges reward: a real problem, a working AI pipeline, and that it's **live**, not a mockup. We hit all three.

---

## PRE-RECORD CHECKLIST (do this first — 15 min)
- [ ] **Two browser profiles / states ready:** (1) logged-out for the landing shot, (2) logged-in on a clean demo account.
- [ ] **Pre-seed ONE strong CV** on the demo account (a construction worker or driver persona) so the "generated CV", templates, PDF, and Job Match beats show polished, complete data — don't gamble on live-day generation quality for those shots.
- [ ] Still **record the voice-recording flow live** (that authenticity is the whole point) — but keep the pre-seeded CV as the thing you open right after, so the cut is clean.
- [ ] **Prepare your spoken answers** (see persona below) and rehearse them 2–3× so they're crisp.
- [ ] Browser at **1920×1080**, zoom 100%, hide bookmarks bar, close other tabs, enable Do-Not-Disturb (no notifications on screen).
- [ ] Good mic, quiet room. Record narration **separately** and lay it over the screen capture if you can — it's far cleaner than talking while clicking.
- [ ] Tools: **OBS Studio** (free) or Loom for capture; any editor (CapCut, DaVinci Resolve free) to trim + add captions.

### Demo persona (Arabic voice input)
**Ahmed**, a construction worker in **Riyadh, Saudi Arabia**. Suggested spoken answers when the app asks:
- *Name & location:* "اسمي أحمد، أعيش في الرياض، المملكة العربية السعودية" (My name is Ahmed, I live in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia)
- *Most recent job:* speak ~2 sentences about site work, safety, teamwork.
- Keep answers short and natural — Whisper handles conversational speech well.

> **Urdu swap:** same script, persona = **a driver in Karachi, Pakistan** speaking Urdu. Everything else is identical. Whichever you can record most naturally will read as more genuine to judges.

## SCENE 1 — The problem (0:00–0:20)
🎬 Open on the **vocv.app landing page** (logged out). Slow scroll past the hero. Let the tagline breathe.

🎙️ *"Millions of skilled workers across the Gulf — builders, drivers, caregivers — can't apply for the jobs they're qualified for. Not because they lack the skills, but because they can't write a CV. Literacy is the barrier. VoiceCV removes it."*

💬 Caption: **"Millions can't apply — because they can't write a CV."**

*(≈45 words)*

---

## SCENE 2 — The solution, in one line (0:20–0:35)
🎬 Click into the app / show the record entry point. Hover the language picker (EN · AR · UR · IT) so all four are visible.

🎙️ *"With VoiceCV, you don't type anything. You just speak — in Arabic, Urdu, English, or Italian — and AI turns your voice into a professional CV in minutes."*

💬 Caption: **"Just speak. In your language. Get a real CV."**

*(≈35 words)*

---

## SCENE 3 — THE HERO MOMENT: voice → CV (0:35–1:25)
🎬 Start the record flow. **Select Arabic** → watch the UI flip **right-to-left**. Show the first question. Tap the big record button, **speak Ahmed's answer** (waveform animating). Advance through 2–3 questions on screen (you can trim the rest). Then show the **processing** state → the **confetti** completion.

🎙️ *"Ahmed picks Arabic — and the whole interface flips to read right-to-left. He hears each question, and simply answers out loud. No forms, no typing. Behind the scenes, his speech is transcribed with Whisper, and Claude structures it into a complete CV — work history, skills, achievements — written in polished, professional English, ready for employers."*

💬 Captions (sequence): **"Pick your language →"** … **"Answer by voice →"** … **"Whisper transcribes · Claude writes the CV"**

*(≈65 words — this is your longest beat; let the visuals carry it, don't rush)*

> ⏱️ **Editing tip:** speed-ramp the middle questions to 1.5–2× so this whole beat lands in ~50s without feeling clipped. Keep the language flip and the confetti at full speed — those are the "wow" frames.

---

## SCENE 4 — It's real: edit + download (1:25–2:05)
🎬 **Cut to the pre-seeded, polished CV** (`/cv/[id]`). Scroll it slowly — clean, complete. Click one field, edit inline to show it's live-editable. Show the **template options** (Modern → a premium one). Click **Download PDF** and show the file opening.

🎙️ *"The result isn't a rough draft — it's a finished CV. Every field is editable by tap. Choose from professional templates, including premium designs. And download a print-ready PDF in one click. This is running live, today, at vocv.app."*

💬 Caption: **"Fully editable · Premium templates · One-click PDF · Live now"**

*(≈45 words)*

---

## SCENE 5 — It goes further: AI Job Match (2:05–2:30)  *(cuttable if over time)*
🎬 Open **/jobs**. Pick the CV → run a search. Show the **ranked job listings** with match scores + the one-line "why it fits" reason. Click **Apply** on one.

🎙️ *"And once you have a CV, VoiceCV finds work. It searches live job listings, and Claude ranks each one against your profile with a reason it fits — then you apply and track your applications, all in the app."*

💬 Caption: **"AI-matched jobs · ranked to your CV · apply & track"**

*(≈40 words)*

> ✂️ **If you're over 3:00, cut this scene entirely** and add one line to Scene 6: *"— it even matches you to live jobs and tracks your applications."*

---

## SCENE 6 — Impact + call to action (2:30–3:00)
🎬 Return to a clean hero shot or a montage of the CV + languages. End on the **vocv.app** URL + "DevVibe AI".

🎙️ *"VoiceCV is live in four languages, built to help millions join the workforce — directly supporting Saudi Vision 2030's goal of getting more people into meaningful jobs. From a voice, to a CV, to a career. Try it now at vocv.app."*

💬 Caption: **"Voice → CV → Career · 4 languages · vocv.app"**

*(≈45 words)*

---

## Total narration ≈ 275 words → ~2:00 spoken, leaving ~1:00 for silent demo action, animations, and breathing room. That's the right ratio — **let the product play on screen; don't talk over every second.**

## Quick self-check before you submit
- [ ] Under 3:00.
- [ ] The **language flip + confetti** are visible and un-rushed (the memorable frames).
- [ ] No personal data / real emails on screen (use the demo persona).
- [ ] Audio levels consistent; no dead air longer than ~2s.
- [ ] Ends on **vocv.app** so judges can try it themselves.
- [ ] Exported 1080p, MP4, ≤ the platform's size limit.

## Variants at a glance
- **Voiceless version:** drop the narration, keep the 💬 captions as on-screen text cards (2–3s each). Same timing.
- **Urdu version:** swap persona to the Karachi driver; everything else identical.
- **2-minute cut** (if required): keep Scenes 1, 3, 4, 6; drop 2 and 5.
